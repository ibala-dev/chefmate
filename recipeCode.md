### Beef Bolognese Pasta

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste

**Instructions:**
1. Boil salted water.
2. Cook ground beef until browned.
3. Add onion and garlic.
4. Add tomatoes and seasonings.
5. Simmer for 15-20 minutes.
6. Cook pasta and combine with sauce.
